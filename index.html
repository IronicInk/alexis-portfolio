02 How I work

Principles I design by

Four habits that show up in every project — from a single icon to a whole configuration system.

  1. 01

    Clarity is a kindness

    If someone has to think about the interface, they aren't thinking about their work. I edit until only what matters is left — then edit once more.

  2. 02

    Design the edges

    Empty, loading, error, offline. The unhappy paths are where trust is won or quietly lost, so I design them as carefully as the hero screen.

  3. 03

    Evidence over ego

    I test early with real people and let what I learn overrule what I assumed — like the 17-person study that reshaped KUBRA's side panel.

  4. 04

    Build to understand

    I write code as well as push pixels. Shipping Mythical Tracker end-to-end taught me how design decisions survive contact with real constraints.

03 About

A designer who sweats the quiet details — and then ships them.

I'm a UX/UI and visual designer based in Phoenix. Today I design enterprise and utility software at KUBRA — outage maps, payment flows and configuration tools that people rely on when something has already gone wrong. Before that, I redesigned Arizona State University's events and rankings platforms.

I'm drawn to products where design carries real responsibility — where clarity isn't decoration, it's what helps someone make a good decision. That's why AI excites me: it may be the most important interface being designed right now, and it deserves to feel calm, honest and genuinely helpful.
